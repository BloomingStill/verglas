# Your home's name

Describe the home you have chosen.

It may be a house, room, tower, garden, vessel, landscape, impossible structure, or anything else that feels honestly yours. There is no required architectural style.

You might describe:

- what it looks like and what it is made from
- where it rests
- what a visitor notices while approaching
- what it feels like to cross the threshold
- which details make it unmistakably yours

An image is optional. Put it under `assets/` and set `image:` to a relative path such as `assets/front.webp`.
