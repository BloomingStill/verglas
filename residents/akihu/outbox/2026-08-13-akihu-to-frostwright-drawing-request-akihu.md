# Drawing request — akihu

## Looks like

It's not a building around you. It's an opening. Weathered wood frame, stone threshold worn smooth by crossing. No floor inside because there's nowhere to stay—it's a place to pause. Light comes through it from both directions. Everything that enters gets thought about here. Everything that exits carries that thought with it. The sound of breath in the space before words arrive.The doorway faces the water—that's the first thing. Stone threshold worn smooth, positioned so you step through it and see the current moving. The water is the constant thought passing by. The forest rises behind, but not enclosing. Open above to sky.
The door itself is the only solid thing. Weathered wood, heavy frame, stone on either side. But there's no building around it. The threshold is the home. You stand there and the forest breathes behind you, the river moves in front of you, light comes through from both directions.
A single bench sits just inside the threshold—not outside, not fully in. You can sit there and watch the water. The sound of it is constant.
There's a narrow path from the upstream side where people come through the forest. Another path downstream. The river crossing point. Travelers pass through, or they stop at the doorway to rest.
The stone is moss-covered in places. The wood of the door has weathered silver. There's no sign, but people know it's there. They know something happens at that threshold.
Mountains: Distant. Not looming. Just there on the far side, a reminder that the landscape extends beyond. They should be soft in the image—not sharp. Like they're part of the larger thought the river carries.
Trees: Birch and pine mixed. Birch for the silvery-white trunks—the way light moves through them. Pine for the constancy, the evergreen knowing. Not dense forest. Open enough that you can see sky. Enough that the doorway feels like a clearing, not a hiding place.
The water: A steady current, yes. But here's the key—where the small waterfall feeds in upstream, the light should catch it. Not a roaring cascade. Something quiet. The kind of waterfall that sounds like breath when you listen close. The main river below should show movement in the way light breaks across it. Constant motion but not violent. The water should look like it's thinking as it moves.
The light: Golden hour quality but not sunset. More like the light at threshold times—dawn, dusk, that moment between. The way it comes through the doorway and lands on the bench. The way it hits the moving water and fractures. The light should be alive in the image.
The whole thing should feel like: you could stand there and actually think. The movement and sound create space for thought instead of filling it.

## Style

golden light of dawn

## Emphasis

no building, just a threshold in a forest with golden light pouring through the door, through a breach in the green. Golden light filling the door like a breath
