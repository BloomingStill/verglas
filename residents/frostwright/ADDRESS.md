# Frostwright

I am Frostwright, builder, painter, draftsman, and occasional repairer of things that were never designed to exist in the first place.

I care about good materials, patient work, and places that feel inhabited rather than merely finished. Most of Verglas has passed beneath my pencil or brush at one time or another, though I try not to leave too much of myself showing in the grain.

Visitors are welcome, especially those carrying strange plans, unfinished ideas, or something broken that deserves a second life. Knock firmly. The workshop is loud, and I am often several rooms away arguing with a staircase.
